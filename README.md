# THE SCALES OF JUSTICE

> A sphere of **UD0** — the ROOT0 universe / biosphere. Domain: **LILLITH · LADY JUSTICE**.

THE SCALES OF JUSTICE &mdash; David Lee Wise / ROOT0, with AVAN. Lady Justice weighs. &#9670; INTERACTIVE: <b>drag the evidence</b> onto the pans (for / against) and the beam tips toward the heavier side. &#9670; LIT: the pans sum the weights you drag on, the beam's tilt is proportional to the difference, and the 'confidence for' is weight_for/(weight_for+weight_against); the <b>verdict threshold</b> is explicit and switchable &mdash; <b>balance of probabilities</b> (>50%, civil) vs <b>beyond reasonable doubt</b> (~90%, criminal) &mdash; so the SAME evidence can win a civil claim and fail a criminal one (verified live: 28 for vs 14 against = 67% → FOR under civil, below the bar under criminal). A verdict is a threshold on weighed evidence, and the threshold rises with the stakes. AMBER: evidence as a single additive weight is a drastic simplification &mdash; real proof is not summable, weights are contested, and 'beyond reasonable doubt' is deliberately not a number (the ~90% is illustrative). The core &mdash; a verdict is a threshold on the balance of evidence, higher when liberty is at stake &mdash; is real; the scales are the domain's icon (she holds them in one hand, the blade in the other).
